# 📊 Domain 1: IT Risk Management & Framework Implementation

## 📋 GRC Domain Scope
* Risk Identification, Analysis, and Evaluation
* System Authorization (ATO) Lifecycle Management
* Remediation Planning & POA&M Tracking

## 🛠️ Implementation Workflow

### 1. The Risk Assessment Lifecycle (NIST SP 800-30 / 800-53)
I led risk management assessments to identify critical system vulnerabilities and translate technical risks into business impact metrics:

```text
[Identify Asset & Threat] ──> [Assess Vulnerability Impact] ──> [Determine Risk Level]
                                                                          │
                                                                          ▼
                                                            [Apply NIST Controls / Mitigation]
```

### 2. Risk Mitigation & Corrective Actions (POA&Ms)
* **Risk Register Entry:** Identified unauthorized account usage and lack of endpoint isolation.
* **Mitigation Strategy:** Documented operational deficiencies and built a **Plan of Action and Milestones (POA&M)** in **eMASS**.
* **Control Testing:** Validated remediation effectiveness before formally presenting residual risk to the agency authorizing official.
