# 🛡️ Domain 2: Compliance Engineering & Audit Readiness

## 📋 GRC Domain Scope
* Control Design & Operating Effectiveness
* Evidence Collection & Artifact Mapping
* Internal & External Audit Support

## 🛠️ Implementation Workflow

### 1. SOC 2 Type II Control Mapping & Validation
I directed continuous control monitoring to validate the **Security** Trust Services Criteria:

* **Access Control Audits:** Extracted user-provisioning artifacts from **Microsoft Entra ID** to prove zero-day termination compliance.
* **System Operations Evidence:** Gathered technical evidence to prove all administrative account actions are logged to a central repository.

### 2. Audit Evidence Lifecycle
* **Automated Evidence Collection:** Built automated pipelines to pull compliance reports periodically.
* **Gap Analysis:** Identified instances where control evidence was missing or outdated, and coordinated with IT engineering teams to remediate before external auditor evaluation.
