# 🚀 NIST RMF & FISMA Risk Assessment (NASA Framework)

## 📋 Business Scenario & Objective
Federal information systems are subject to strict **FISMA** requirements and must maintain a formal Authority to Operate (ATO) using the **NIST Risk Management Framework (RMF / SP 800-37)**. 

**The Challenge:** Assessing over 10 mission-critical systems across 200+ individual controls without interrupting active agency operations.
**The Solution:** I led the technical control validation, continuous monitoring, and evidence package development within **eMASS** to secure and maintain federal system authorizations.

---

## 🛠️ Implementation & Evidence Collection

### 1. Control Selection & Scoping (NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5)
I executed technical scoping for federal systems, explicitly documenting the status of security controls across these critical domains:
* **AC (Access Control):** Enforced account management, least privilege, and session termination controls.
* **AU (Audit and Accountability):** Validated that audit records contain sufficient information to establish what events occurred.
* **IA (Identification and Authentication):** Validated organizational user identification and certificate-based authentication (MFA).

### 2. Risk Assessment Methodology in eMASS
* **Artifact Collection:** Conducted stakeholder interviews and technical configuration audits to evaluate control execution.
* **POA&M Creation:** Documented critical control deviations and drafted actionable **Plans of Action and Milestones (POA&M)** to track mitigation steps.
* **Residual Risk Analysis:** Performed impact analyses to determine the operational risk of non-compliant configurations prior to agency head sign-off.

---

## 📉 Business Value & Risk Reduction
* **95% Validation Accuracy:** Achieved rapid, verifiable control sign-offs across 10 agency systems.
* **Continuous ATO State:** Eliminated authorization expiration risks by operationalizing standard continuous monitoring plans.
